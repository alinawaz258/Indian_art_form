## 1. Primary Canons & Classical Treatises Consulted
1. **Chitrasutra of the Vishnudharmottara Purana** (c. 5th–7th century CE) — The canonical Sanskrit foundation on Indian painting principles, surface preparations, and proportion systems (*pramanas*).
2. **Shilparatna** by Sreekumara (16th century CE) — Traditional manual governing South Indian temple mural fresco-secco techniques and the *Pancha-varna* pigment classification.
3. **Abhilashitartha Chintamani / Manasollasa** by Western Chalukya King Someshvara III (12th century CE) — Encyclopedic Sanskrit treatise documenting courtly arts, miniature pigments, and metal casting.

## 2. Museum Catalogs & Institutional Repositories
1. **National Museum, New Delhi:**
   - Harappan Gallery: Accession records for the *Dancing Girl* (Hr. 5721/195) and *Pashupati Seal* (DK 5175/143).
   - Miniature Painting Collection: Rajasthani, Mughal, and Pahari folios (*Bani Thani*, *Radha in the Grove*).
2. **Archaeological Survey of India (ASI):**
   - Sarnath Museum: *Lion Capital of Ashoka* (Acc. No. 355) and *Seated Preaching Buddha* (Acc. No. 343).
   - World Heritage Monographs: *Ajanta Caves*, *Bhimbetka Rock Shelters*, *Sun Temple Konark*, *Hampi Monuments*, *Mahabalipuram*.
3. **Victoria and Albert Museum, London:**
   - South Asian Collection: *Akbar Taming Hawai* (IS.2:21-1896), *Kalamkari Temple Cloths* (IS.34-1985), *Kalighat Paintings* (W.G. Archer Collection).
4. **Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York:**
   - Department of Asian Art: Gandharan Schist Sculptures and Chola Dynasty Bronzes (*Shiva Nataraja*).
5. **Government Museum, Chennai (Egmore):**
   - Bronze Gallery: *Tiruvelangadu Nataraja* (Acc. No. 236) and Pallava stone carvings.
6. **Bihar Museum & Patna Museum:**
   - Archaeological Collection: *Didarganj Yakshi* (Acc. No. Arch 134).
7. **Geographical Indications (GI) Registry of India:**
   - Official filings for: Madhubani Painting (GI No. 45), Thanjavur Painting (GI No. 33), Srikalahasti Kalamkari (GI No. 19), Odisha Pattachitra (GI No. 86), Warli Painting (GI No. 436), Cheriyal Paintings (GI No. 71), Kani Shawl (GI No. 46), Rogan Art of Kutch (GI No. 593).
