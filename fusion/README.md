## 2. Executive Rationale & Selected Synthesis
- **Selected Traditions:** **Madhubani (Mithila) Painting (Bihar) × Gond Tribal Art (Madhya Pradesh)**
- **Artwork Title:** *Vriksha-Chaitanya* (Consciousness of the Cosmic Tree)
- **Conceptual Synergy:** Fuses Mithila’s geometric fertility symbolism (the nuptial *Kohbar*, *Matsya* fish, *Kamala* lotus) with the Gond tribe’s animistic worldview (*Jiva* residing in all flora and fauna).
- **Structural Synthesis:**
  - *Terrestrial Base (Madhubani):* Double-line contours, *kachni* linear hatching, paired fish, and blooming lotus rhizomes.
  - *Arboreal Canopy (Gond):* Fluid biomorphic branches, antlered spirit deer, morphing birds, and signature optical stippling (*bindu*, *danna*).
  - *Central Trunk (The Synthesis Axis):* Gradual metamorphic transition where Madhubani geometric chevrons dissolve into Gond vertical wood-grain stippling.
  - *Integrated Border:* Outer edge features Madhubani triangular teeth (*daant*); inner edge features Gond wave-and-dot ribbons.
