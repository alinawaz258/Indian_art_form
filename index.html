कला-वृत्त — The Wheel and Continuous Cycle of Indian Art

A comprehensive digital exhibition and research repository addressing the CLA-I Assessment for 21LEM301T: Indian Art Form. Three integrated projects articulate the development of Indian art across Time (When), Space (Where), and Synthesis (Creative Union).

Course: 21LEM301T — Indian Art Form Assessment: CLA-I (Weightage: 30 Marks) Outcomes: CO1 (Timeline & Map) + CO2 (Fusion) Status: Complete Academic Submission
Project 1 · 10 Marks · CO1
AXIS: TEMPORAL (WHEN)

Interactive Timeline

Chronological journey across 10 historical epochs from 30,000 BCE to the contemporary era. Features 32 curated artifacts with high-resolution visual cards, category filters, and in-depth scholarly context.

  • ✓ 10 Epochs: Prehistoric to Progressive Modernists

Architecture & Local Verification

Zero-Dependency Philosophy

Built using pure semantic HTML5, CSS3, and ES6 JavaScript. No npm builds, external database connections, paid APIs, or fragile map tile services are required. The entire suite runs directly from the local filesystem or any standard web server.

Shared Data Layer

Both the Timeline and Art Map are powered by a unified, verified dataset (shared/data/artData.js), ensuring consistent dates, terminology, museum citations, and geographic coordinates across interfaces.
