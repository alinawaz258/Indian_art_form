Kalāvṛtta

कला-वृत्त — the living wheel of Indian art

A digital exhibition tracing Indian visual culture from prehistoric cave paintings to contemporary modernist practice. The archive follows three movements: when art emerged, where it flourished, and how regional traditions continue to speak to one another.

Curatorial themes

Time

Continuity

From rock shelter paintings to temple sculpture and modernist abstraction, Indian art evolves through recurring motifs, ritual memory, and regional continuity.

Place

Landscape

Geography shapes medium, iconography, and community practice, from Himalayan miniatures to coastal temple towns and forest-based tribal traditions.

Synthesis

Exchange

Regional styles are not isolated; they share recurring concerns with ecology, devotion, memory, and visual rhythm, producing new interpretations.
